## Replace Non-Reasons

AI occupies the reason slot without giving a reason. The phrase implies the cause is known.

**AI pattern:**
> The cache is skipped for various reasons, due to the nature of the system. This is by design.

**Human alternative:**
> The cache is skipped because webhook payloads can arrive out of order.

**Words to watch:** for various/obvious/a variety of reasons, due to the nature of, for performance reasons, for safety reasons, for legacy reasons, as one would expect, this is by design

**Fix:** Give the actual reason. If it is unknown, say so. Vague attribution (`content-vague-attribution`) invents experts. This invents a cause.
