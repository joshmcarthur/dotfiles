## Cut Self-Praise

AI rates the work instead of explaining it. The sentence asserts a virtue the reader cannot check.

**AI pattern:**
> This ensures correctness and handles the edge case gracefully, following best practices for readability.

**Human alternative:**
> Reject payloads with a missing `user_id`. Without this check, the job raises `NoMethodError` on `nil`.

**Words to watch:** ensures correctness, handles gracefully/elegantly/seamlessly, best practices, for readability, for clarity, more maintainable, the cleanest/simplest/most elegant way, this ensures that

**Fix:** State what breaks without the line, or what the code/text actually does. Promotional language (`content-promotional`) is brochure tone. This is the sentence congratulating itself.
