## Cut Process Narration

AI announces the next action, a duty to mention something, or what it can see. State the result instead.

**AI pattern:**
> Let me check the logs. I can see the issue is in line 42. I should mention that the timeout is 30 seconds.

**Human alternative:**
> Line 42 sets a 30-second timeout. After that, the client retries.

**Words to watch:** let me check/analyze/review/explain, I'll analyze/walk through, I'm going to examine, I need to note/mention, I should mention, I can see that, I can tell you need

**Fix:** Drop the tour of your next step. Keep the observation. Chatbot wrappers (`comm-artifacts`) are the envelope ("I hope this helps"). This is the body narrating itself.
