## Avoid Colon Reveals

AI uses a capitalized setup, a colon, and a lowercase punchline. The colon does theatrical work that a sentence should do.

**AI pattern:**
> The detail that makes it work: a separate agent grades the output.

**Human alternative:**
> A separate agent grades the output. That is what makes it work.

**Pattern to watch:** A line that opens with a capitalized phrase, a colon, then a lowercase continuation. Keep colons for lists, labels, and quotes. Keep admonition labels (`Note:`, `Tip:`, `Warning:`).

**Fix:** Rewrite as a plain sentence. Prefatory hooks like "Here's the thing:" belong to `filler-openings`. This is the general reveal shape.
