## Avoid Templated Headings

AI reaches for listicle titles that could sit on any article. The heading does not say what the section covers.

**AI pattern:**
> ## Why This Matters
> ## A Deep Dive
> ## Key Takeaways
> ## Understanding Webhooks

**Human alternative:**
> ## What a missed webhook does
> ## Strava retry behaviour
> ## What to check after deploy

**Words to watch:** Why X Matters, Understanding X, Demystifying, Unlocking, Unleashing, Mastering, A Deep Dive, The Ultimate Guide, Everything You Need to Know, N Reasons/Ways/Tips, Best Practices, Key Takeaways, The X Behind

**Fix:** Name the section's actual contents. Inline-header lists (`style-inline-headers`) are `**Label:**` bullets. This is the heading above them.
