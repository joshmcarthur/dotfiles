## Replace Metaphor with Mechanism

AI explains by analogy instead of naming the thing. Every word is English; none of them say what happens.

**AI pattern:**
> Think of this as a load-bearing seam. The adapter acts as a first-class citizen in the request path.

**Human alternative:**
> The adapter sits on the request path. If it is missing, auth is skipped.

**Words to watch:** think of this/it as, reads as the, acts as a, behaves like a, akin to, analogous to, load-bearing (outside construction), provenance (outside supply-chain/build), first-class citizen, the X equivalent of, in the same way that

**Fix:** Delete the analogy. If the reader lost the reason, rewrite with the mechanism, the constraint, or the failure mode. If they lost nothing, it was decoration.

Do not ban useful comparisons. Ban comparisons that stand in for an explanation.
